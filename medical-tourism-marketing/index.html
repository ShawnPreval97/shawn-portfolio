The work

See which ads turn into real conversations

Many clinics and medical travel companies already spend on ads. The hard part is knowing which inquiries are serious, whether anyone followed up, and whether the spend is worth continuing. Reports that stop at clicks do not answer that.

What Shawn does

  • Reviews the account, offer, and how inquiries reach your team.
  • Runs and improves Meta and Google ads.
  • Connects inquiries to tracking so you can see what is working.
  • Sends clear updates on spend, inquiry quality, and next steps.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is this service for?
Clinics and medical travel companies, including cosmetic, dental, hair-restoration, fertility, and established medical-travel providers. It is not aimed at primary care, hospitals, or urgent care.
Which advertising platforms do you work with?
Shawn typically manages Meta ads and Google Ads, including work such as cosmetic surgery advertising and medical tourism marketing when that is the client’s focus.
Who owns the advertising accounts?
You should own the Meta, Google Ads, and tracking accounts. Shawn works inside those accounts.
Who handles creative?
Shawn can write ad copy and offer messaging. Your photos, video, and brand assets are used when you have them.
How is measurement handled?
Tracking is connected so you can see which ads produce inquiries and which of those inquiries become serious consultation requests.
How does pricing work?
Pricing depends on the accounts, spend, and tracking work involved. Shawn discusses scope after an advertising review. There is no published fixed retainer.
Does Shawn provide medical advice?
No. Shawn handles advertising and measurement, not medical advice or treatment arrangements.
